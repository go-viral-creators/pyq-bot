Privacy Policy

AuraMic AI is built so that your voice stays on your phone.

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Who we are

AuraMic AI (“the app”) is published by FireClash Pro (“we”, “us”). Contact: support@fireclashpro.com.

2. The short version

  • All recording, noise removal and encoding happen on your device.
  • We do not collect, upload, sell or share your audio, messages, contacts or other personal data.
  • The app has no account system, no advertising SDKs and no analytics SDKs.
  • The app needs no internet connection to work.

3. Data the app handles

DataWhyWhere it goes

The cleaned file is written to the app's private cache, shared with WhatsApp only when you send, and deleted automatically (about 10 minutes after sharing; leftovers are purged on next start). Studio Lab audio is never saved to disk.
Name of the open chat (read through the Accessibility service)To select the same chat in WhatsApp's share screenHeld in memory for the few seconds the hand-off takes; never stored or transmitted.
Foreground app name (Usage access)To show the floating mic only in WhatsApp / WhatsApp BusinessEvaluated on the device; never stored or transmitted.
App settings (language, noise strength, bubble position, counters of cleaned notes)To remember your preferences and show your statsStored locally on your device only. Excluded from cloud backup.

4. Permissions

  • Display over other apps — draws the floating mic.
  • Microphone — records while you hold the button.
  • Usage access — detects whether WhatsApp is in the foreground.
  • Accessibility service — taps Send inside WhatsApp; see the Accessibility Disclaimer.
  • Notifications / foreground service — shows the status notification that Android requires while the microphone service runs.
  • Vibration — optional haptic feedback.

5. Third parties

We do not share data with third parties. Files you choose to send are handed to WhatsApp, which processes them under its own terms and privacy policy. AuraMic AI is not affiliated with WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc.

6. Children

The app is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect their data (we collect no personal data at all).

7. Security

Because audio never leaves your device, there is no server-side copy to protect. Temporary files live in the app's private storage and are shared through a time-limited content URI granted only to WhatsApp.

8. Your rights

Since we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Uninstalling the app removes all local data. If you believe we hold data about you, email support@fireclashpro.com.

9. Changes

If this policy changes we will update the date above and, for material changes, tell you in the app or on the Google Play listing.
